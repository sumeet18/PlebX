Privacy Policy

We believe people should be able to understand how their information may be handled when they interact with PlebX.

Draft for review: This is a cautious general draft based on the supplied landing page. Actual forms, app, analytics, hosting, cookies, payment flows, vendors and retention periods have not been provided. Verify and complete this policy against real data practices and applicable Indian privacy requirements before publishing.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy describes how PLEBAI LLP, operating the PlebX brand (“PlebX”, “we”, “us” or “our”), may handle personal information when you visit the website, contact us, submit an interest form or use PlebX services.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on how you interact with PlebX, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, service requirements, professional or business details, and the contents of messages or enquiries. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless specifically requested through an appropriate, secure process.

3. Information collected through use

When configured to do so, the website or app may collect technical information such as device and browser details, IP address, approximate usage information and diagnostic or security logs. Cookies or similar technologies may also be used if enabled. PlebX should identify the exact tools and technologies it actually uses.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used, as relevant to the feature you choose, to:

  • respond to enquiries and requests to register interest;
  • create, maintain or support Seeker and Provider profiles or connections;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website and platform;
  • send service-related updates and important notices;
  • prevent misuse, investigate complaints and comply with legal obligations.

Marketing communications should be sent only in accordance with applicable law and required consent or opt-out choices.

5. When information may be shared

Depending on the feature and your choices, information may be shared with another user where needed to facilitate a requested connection, with service providers supporting the platform, or with public authorities and other parties where required or permitted by law. PlebX should identify its actual processors, integrations and recipient categories before this policy is finalised.

6. Security and retention

PlebX should apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information it handles and retain personal information only as long as needed for the stated purpose or as required by law. Specific security measures, retention periods, deletion processes and backup practices must be confirmed against PLEBAI LLP’s actual systems; this draft does not claim any particular measure is currently in place.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law, identity verification where reasonably necessary and lawful retention obligations. PlebX should publish a clear privacy-request and grievance process before launch.

8. Children

The website and services are not intended to invite children to submit personal information without required involvement or consent of a parent or lawful guardian. PlebX should confirm its minimum-age and parental-consent approach for each service before launch.

9. Third-party services

The website may link to third-party services, including forms or social platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before providing information to them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as services and data practices change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact PLEBAI LLP at plebx@plebx.in. Add designated privacy contact or grievance officer details here if required by applicable law.
