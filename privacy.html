1. Introduction

Welcome to PlebX.

At PlebX, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the PlebX mobile application, website, and related services (collectively referred to as the “Platform”).

We encourage you to read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the Platform.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Where required under applicable law, your continued use of the Platform or your explicit consent shall constitute your agreement to the processing of your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • visitors to the Platform;

  • registered users;

  • Seekers;

  • Providers;

  • individuals communicating with PlebX through the Platform or official support channels.

This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected by PlebX through its official Platform and does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked from the Platform.

3. Our Privacy Principles

PlebX is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

Our privacy practices are guided by the following principles:

  • We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate and improve the Platform.

  • We use personal information only for legitimate and lawful purposes.

  • We implement reasonable safeguards to protect personal information.

  • We do not sell users’ personal information.

  • We respect users’ rights under applicable laws.

  • We continuously review and improve our privacy and security practices.

4. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

Personal Information means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

Processing includes collecting, storing, recording, organising, using, analysing, sharing, transmitting, deleting, or otherwise handling personal information.

Platform means the PlebX mobile application, website, and related services.

User means any individual accessing or using the Platform.

Seeker and Provider have the meanings given to them in our Terms of Use.

We, Us, Our, or PlebX means the entity operating the Platform.

5. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following categories of information.

5.1 Information You Provide

This may include:

  • Full Name

  • Mobile Number

  • Date of Birth

  • Gender

  • Profile Photograph

  • Selfie

  • Bio

  • Languages

  • Skills

  • Categories

  • Educational Information

  • Professional Experience

  • Location preferences

  • Radius preferences

  • Support requests

  • Feedback

  • Reviews

  • Ratings

  • Information voluntarily shared through messages or profile updates

5.2 Verification Information

Where verification is required, we may collect information necessary to verify your identity or profile.

Verification may include:

  • Selfie

  • Identity verification information

  • Mobile number verification

  • Other verification information introduced from time to time.

Verification is intended to improve trust and safety on the Platform.

5.3 Device Information

We may automatically collect technical information including:

  • Device model

  • Operating System

  • App Version

  • Device Identifier

  • Language settings

  • Time zone

  • IP Address

  • Network information

  • Crash reports

  • Performance logs

This information helps us improve Platform performance and security.

5.4 Usage Information

We may collect information regarding how you use the Platform, including:

  • Login activity

  • Feature usage

  • Search activity

  • Profile views

  • Messages sent through the Platform

  • User preferences

  • Subscription status

  • Interaction with notifications

This information helps us improve user experience.

5.5 Location Information

Where you grant permission, we may collect your location information to:

  • show nearby users;

  • improve search results;

  • provide location-based features;

  • improve Platform functionality.

You may manage location permissions through your device settings.

5.6 Payment Information

If you purchase subscriptions or Premium Features, payments are processed through authorised third-party payment providers.

PlebX generally does not store your complete payment card information.

We may retain limited transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction ID

  • Payment Status

  • Subscription Details

  • Purchase Date

  • Invoice Information

5.7 Communications

When you communicate with us, we may retain:

  • Customer support requests

  • Emails

  • Complaint details

  • Feedback

  • Survey responses

This information helps us improve our services and respond to user requests.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for purposes that are reasonably connected to operating the Platform and providing our services to you. These purposes include:

  • creating, verifying, and managing your Account;
  • displaying your Profile to other users and enabling discovery based on categories, skills, location, and other preferences;
  • facilitating communication between Seekers and Providers;
  • showing nearby users and location-based results, where you have enabled location access;
  • processing subscriptions, Premium Features, and related transactions;
  • providing customer support and responding to your requests, complaints, and feedback;
  • sending service-related communications, such as OTPs, account alerts, and important updates;
  • detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud, fake profiles, abuse, and other misuse;
  • reviewing reports and enforcing our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines;
  • maintaining the security, stability, and performance of the Platform;
  • analysing usage trends to improve features and user experience;
  • complying with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, and lawful requests from authorities; and
  • establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

PlebX does not routinely monitor private messages between users. We may review, preserve, or disclose messages only where reasonably necessary to investigate a reported violation, protect the safety of users, or comply with applicable law.

If we wish to use your personal information for a purpose that is materially different from those described in this Privacy Policy, we will inform you and, where required, seek your consent.

7. Basis for Processing

We process your personal information:

  • on the basis of the consent you provide when you register for and use the Platform;
  • for purposes for which you have voluntarily provided your information, such as when you contact customer support;
  • where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws, court orders, or directions of competent authorities; and
  • for any other legitimate use permitted under applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described in Section 12. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

8. How We Share Your Information

PlebX does not sell users’ personal information.

We share personal information only in the following circumstances:

8.1 With Other Users

Information that you include in your Profile, such as your name, photograph, bio, languages, skills, categories, experience, ratings, reviews, verification status, and approximate location or distance, may be visible to other users of the Platform. Information you share in messages is visible to the users you communicate with.

Please consider carefully what you choose to share publicly or with other users.

8.2 With Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third parties who process it on our behalf, such as:

  • cloud hosting and storage providers;
  • verification and authentication service providers;
  • payment gateways and payment service providers;
  • SMS, OTP, email, and notification service providers;
  • analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring providers; and
  • customer support tools.

These service providers may use personal information only to perform services for PlebX and are required to protect it in accordance with applicable law and their contractual obligations.

8.3 For Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or lawful requests from government or law enforcement authorities;
  • enforce our Terms of Use and other policies;
  • investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse of the Platform; or
  • protect the rights, property, or safety of PlebX, our users, or the public.

8.4 Business Transfers

If PlebX is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law. The recipient will be required to continue protecting your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy or a policy offering comparable protection.

8.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information with other parties where you have instructed us or given your consent to do so.

9. Data Storage and Transfers

Your personal information is stored on secure servers operated by PlebX or by our cloud service providers.

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside India. Any such transfer will be carried out in accordance with applicable law, including any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide the Platform and maintain your Account;
  • fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy;
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations;
  • resolve disputes and enforce our Terms of Use; and
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and repeat misuse of the Platform.

When you delete your Account or when retention is no longer necessary, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, unless we are required or permitted by law to retain it for longer. Residual copies may remain in backup systems for a limited period before being overwritten.

11. Data Security

PlebX implements reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational security measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, loss, or destruction. These measures may include encryption, access controls, secure infrastructure, and regular monitoring.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can help protect your Account by keeping your mobile number and devices secure and never sharing your OTP with anyone. PlebX will never ask for your OTP or password through messages or calls.

In the event of a personal data breach, PlebX will take appropriate steps to contain the breach and will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required under applicable law.

12. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a summary of the personal information we process about you and the processing activities undertaken;
  • Correction and updating: correct inaccurate or misleading information, complete incomplete information, and update your information. Much of your Profile can be edited directly within the Platform;
  • Erasure: request deletion of your Account and personal information, subject to any retention required or permitted by law;
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent that you have previously given. Withdrawing consent may mean that you can no longer use some or all features of the Platform;
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint about how we handle your personal information; and
  • Nomination: nominate another individual to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity, as permitted under applicable law.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 18. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request. We will respond within the time period required under applicable law.

If you are not satisfied with our response to your grievance, you may have the right to approach the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with applicable law.

13. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is intended only for individuals who are at least 18 years of age.

PlebX does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a person under 18 has registered on the Platform or provided personal information, we will take steps to delete the Account and the associated information.

If you believe a person under 18 is using the Platform, please contact us.

14. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and app may use cookies, software development kits (SDKs), and similar technologies to keep the Platform working, remember your preferences, maintain security, and understand how the Platform is used.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings and manage app permissions through your device settings. Disabling some of these technologies may affect how the Platform functions.

15. Third-Party Services and Links

The Platform may contain links to, or integrate with, third-party websites, applications, payment providers, and services.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before using their services.

16. Communications from PlebX

We may send you service-related communications, including OTPs, account notifications, security alerts, subscription updates, and important changes to our policies. These are necessary for the operation of your Account.

Where we send promotional communications, you may opt out at any time through the Platform, by using the unsubscribe option provided, or by contacting us. Opting out of promotional communications will not stop service-related communications.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

PlebX may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in applicable law, technology, business practices, or Platform features.

The updated version will be published on the Platform with a revised “Last Updated” date. Where changes are material, we will inform you through appropriate means and, where required by applicable law, seek your fresh consent.

18. Grievance Officer and Contact

If you have any questions, requests, or complaints relating to this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal information, please contact our Grievance Officer at plebx@plebx.in.

To help us resolve your request quickly, please include your name, registered mobile number, and a clear description of your request or concern. Please do not share OTPs, passwords, or complete identity document details by email.

We will acknowledge and resolve grievances within the timelines prescribed under applicable law.

19. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the rules made under them.
