Our story

About PlebX

PlebX brings people, services and opportunities together — helping Seekers find the help they need and Providers showcase the skills and services they offer.

Who we are

PlebX is a platform being developed to bring people, services and opportunities together. It is designed to help people who are looking for assistance connect with individuals or businesses offering relevant services.

What PlebX aims to do

PlebX brings two sides of a service connection together:

  • For Seekers: discover help for household needs, professional services, care and wellness, repairs, transport and other everyday requirements.
  • For Providers: showcase skills and services, become more discoverable to potential customers, connect with seekers and grow business opportunities.

Our approach

Our guiding idea is simple: Find • Match • Connect. We aim to make it easier for people to find relevant services and for service providers to present what they do.
